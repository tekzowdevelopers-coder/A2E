Low Tension Switchboards

Dedicated Quality LT Panels

High performance Power Control Centres (PCC), Motor Control Centres (MCC), and APFC capacitor panels rated up to 6300A, 415V.

Product Overview & Engineering Scope

A2E Power System engineers and manufactures premium Dedicated Quality LT Panels adhering strictly to Indian Electricity Rules (IER) and international standard specifications.

A2E Power System manufactures a comprehensive range of custom low-tension (LT) electrical panels tailored for manufacturing plants, heavy machinery, and commercial complexes:

Power Control Centre (PCC)

Central switchboards with air circuit breaker (ACB) incomers up to 6300A, automated bus couplers, and precision power monitoring.

Motor Control Centre (MCC)

Fixed and draw-out motor starter feeders with DOL, Star-Delta, Soft Starter, and VFD integration with overload relays.

APFC Capacitor Panels

Microprocessor-based automatic power factor controllers with detuned harmonic filter reactors to keep PF near unity (0.99).

Distribution Boards (PDB/LDB)

Power distribution boards and lighting boards engineered for balanced phase loading, modular expansion, and high short-circuit withstand.
