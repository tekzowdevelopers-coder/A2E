About A2E Power System

Delivering High-Quality Electrical Solutions Across India

"A2E Power System" supports electrical contractors, cable tray installers, and panel builders. Established in the year 2018 with an ambition to fulfill the comprehensive needs of customers requiring specialized services in the electrical field.

Technical Rigor

Our Engineering & Manufacturing Capabilities

Equipped with modern machinery, testing equipment, and engineering expertise to guarantee superior reliability.

Panel Fabrication & Assembly

In-house CRCA sheet metal cutting, CNC punching, precision bending, multi-tank chemical pre-treatment, and high-durability epoxy polyester powder coating.

Busbar Engineering

High-conductivity ETP grade electrolytic copper and aluminum busbars designed with heat shrinkable color-coded insulation sleeves and torque-tightened joints.

High Voltage Testing

Rigorous dielectric high voltage (HV) insulation testing, secondary current injection, megger insulation resistance testing, and contact resistance checks.

Retrofitting & Overhauls

Seamless retrofitting of obsolete air and vacuum circuit breakers with contemporary high-efficiency breakers without needing complete panel replacements.

Safety & Compliance

Full compliance with IS 8623 / IEC 61439 standards, safety interlocks, door defeat mechanisms, earthing bus continuity, and CEIG inspection clearance.

24*7 Rapid Site Support

Round-the-clock emergency mobilization team for industrial breakdown services, breaker trip diagnostics, cable fault rectification, and power restoration.
