About A2E Power System

Our motto is: "Customer Satisfaction at Any Cost"

Established 2018

Who is A2E Power System?

"A2E Power System" supports electrical contractors, cable tray installers, and panel builders. Established in the year 2018 with an ambition to deliver the comprehensive needs of customers requiring specialized services in the "Electrical Field".

For years, we have provided premium quality service and customer satisfaction in all our works. We carry out electrical installation and panel works, earthing, lighting, LT & HT products sale and service for large-scale industries and are widely involved in manufacturing panel boards, control panels, bus ducts, cable trays, and solar structures. Starting from Hosur, we have extended our quality service across major cities in India, including Trichy, Chennai, and Madurai.

We have a strong team of experienced engineers, supervisors, technicians, and well-trained labor. Our professionals work diligently to ensure flawless services & customer satisfaction at all times.
