Circuit Breakers Service & Sales

Quality VCB & ACB Breakers

Expert supply, overhauling, contact resistance testing, and retrofitting for Air Circuit Breakers (ACB) and Vacuum Circuit Breakers (VCB).

Product Overview & Engineering Scope

A2E Power System engineers and manufactures premium Quality VCB & ACB Breakers adhering strictly to Indian Electricity Rules (IER) and international standard specifications.

Proper maintenance of ACB and VCB breakers is critical to preventing catastrophic plant shutdowns and electrical fires. A2E Power System provides end-to-end service:

  • Contact Resistance Measurement (CRM): Micro-ohm testing of main contacts to prevent localized overheating under high current.
  • Timing & Motion Analysis: Verification of open, close, and simultaneous phase contact operations down to milliseconds.
  • Secondary Current Injection: Calibrating electronic and microprocessor trip releases for overload, short circuit, and earth fault thresholds.
  • Overhaul & Lubrication: Full disassembly, cleaning of arc chutes, spring charging mechanism lubrication, and auxiliary contact dressing.
