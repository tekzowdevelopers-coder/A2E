Cable Management Systems

Perforated & Ladder Cable Trays

Heavy-duty cable management systems including perforated trays, ladder trays, raceways, and support structural channels.

Product Overview & Engineering Scope

A2E Power System engineers and manufactures premium Perforated & Ladder Cable Trays adhering strictly to Indian Electricity Rules (IER) and international standard specifications.

A2E Power System manufactures rugged cable tray solutions engineered to organize, protect, and support heavy power and instrumentation cables:

  • Ladder Type Trays: Unsurpassed ventilation for heavy multi-core power cables with welded or mechanically swaged rungs spaced at 250mm or 300mm centers.
  • Perforated Cable Trays: Continuous uniform punched base providing smooth support for power and control cables while promoting natural air circulation.
  • High Load Bearing Capability: Engineered to withstand heavy cable weight over long spans without sagging or deflection.
  • Complete Accessory Family: Factory fabricated bends, tees, and cross junctions ensuring smooth radius transitions that protect cable jackets from sharp edges.
