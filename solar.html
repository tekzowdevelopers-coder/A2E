Solar Panels & Supports

High-efficiency solar PV modules and heavy-duty Hot-Dip Galvanized (HDG) mounting structures for industrial rooftops and ground arrays.

Product Overview & Engineering Scope

A2E Power System engineers and manufactures premium Solar Panels & Supports adhering strictly to Indian Electricity Rules (IER) and international standard specifications.

Clean renewable solar power starts with an uncompromising structural foundation and precision electrical integration:

  • Hot-Dip Galvanized Channels & Rafters: Precision pre-punched C & Z purlins hot-dipped to survive coastal and industrial corrosive atmospheres.
  • Non-Penetrating Rooftop Clamps: Specialized standing seam and trapezoidal sheet clamps preventing water leakage and protecting factory roof warranties.
  • Solar Combiner Boxes (DCDB / ACDB): Incorporating IP65 polycarbonate enclosures with high-voltage DC fuses, disconnect switches, and type-2 SPDs.
  • Turnkey Solar Integration: Cable sizing, solar inverter connection, bi-directional net metering compliance, and statutory grid synchronization.
